# Failures

Everyone publishes the airplane that flew. Almost nobody publishes the three
motor mounts that cracked first, and that is the part that saves the next person
a year.

Log everything that did not work: parts remade, bond lines that failed a peel
test, dimensions that were wrong, tools that were the wrong choice, and hours
spent on approaches that got abandoned.

Format: date, what was attempted, what happened, what was done instead.
